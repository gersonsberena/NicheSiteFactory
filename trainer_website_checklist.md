# Personal Trainer/Coach Website - Complete Checklist

## Overview
A professional trainer website typically requires **4-7 main pages** depending on complexity. Below is a complete breakdown of essential (must-have) and nice-to-have elements.

---

## 📱 ESSENTIAL PAGES (Minimum 4 pages)

### 1. **Home/Landing Page**
- [ ] **Logo** (clickable, returns to home)
- [ ] **Logo Icon/Favicon** (appears in browser tab)
- [ ] Hero section with compelling headline
- [ ] Subheadline/tagline (what you specialize in)
- [ ] Call-to-action button (e.g., "Book a Free Consultation", "Get Started")
- [ ] Brief intro/about snippet (100-150 words max)
- [ ] Services overview (2-4 key offerings)
- [ ] Testimonials carousel or featured testimonial
- [ ] Social media icons (links in footer or header)
- [ ] Navigation menu (clear and accessible)
- [ ] Footer with copyright, links, contact info

### 2. **About Me Page**
- [ ] **Full name** (prominent)
- [ ] **Professional photo** (high-quality headshot/action shot)
- [ ] Detailed biography (background, experience, credentials)
- [ ] **Certifications and qualifications** (list all relevant certs)
- [ ] Personal story/why you became a trainer
- [ ] Philosophy or coaching approach
- [ ] Specialty areas/niches
- [ ] Education and training background
- [ ] Years of experience
- [ ] **Professional credentials badge** (ACE, NASM, ISSA, etc.)
- [ ] Downloadable bio/media kit (nice-to-have)

### 3. **Services/Programs Page**
- [ ] **Clear list of all services offered**
  - [ ] One-on-one training
  - [ ] Group classes
  - [ ] Online coaching
  - [ ] Nutrition coaching
  - [ ] Specialized programs (weight loss, strength, etc.)
- [ ] Brief description of each service
- [ ] Pricing (or "contact for pricing")
- [ ] Duration/package options
- [ ] Who each service is best for
- [ ] Call-to-action for each service
- [ ] Comparison table (if multiple packages exist) - *nice-to-have*

### 4. **Contact/Book a Call Page**
- [ ] **Phone number** (clickable, prominent)
- [ ] **Email address** (clickable)
- [ ] **Contact form** (name, email, message, phone)
- [ ] Calendar booking widget (Calendly, Acuity, etc.) - *essential if taking bookings*
- [ ] Location/address (if operating from a physical location)
- [ ] Business hours
- [ ] Response time expectation
- [ ] Map embed (Google Maps) - *nice-to-have*
- [ ] Alternative contact methods

## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Make booking easy** - Your #1 priority should be reducing friction to booking. Use Calendly, Acuity Scheduling, or similar tools.

2. **Use social proof aggressively** - Testimonials, reviews, and before/after photos are your biggest conversion tools.

3. **Professional photos matter** - Invest in a professional photoshoot. Phone photos look unprofessional.

4. **Update regularly** - A blog, gallery, or testimonials section that's updated regularly shows you're active and established.

5. **Mobile first** - Most people will visit on mobile. Design for mobile first, then scale up.

6. **Clear value proposition** - Within 5 seconds, a visitor should understand what you do and who you help.

7. **Eliminate friction** - Minimize form fields, simplify navigation, make CTAs obvious.

8. **Trust signals** - Certifications, credentials, client testimonials, years of experience - display them prominently.

9. **Video is powerful** - Even a simple intro video or exercise demo video increases conversion significantly.

10. **SEO matters** - Optimize your site for local search ("personal trainer near me") and fitness-related keywords.
