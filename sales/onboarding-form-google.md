## GOOGLE FORMS SETTINGS CHECKLIST

- [ ] Turn on **"Collect email addresses"** (Settings → Responses) — auto-captures their email
- [ ] Turn on **"Send responders a copy"** — client gets a copy of what they submitted
- [ ] Turn on **"Restrict to 1 response"** — prevents duplicate submissions
- [ ] Set form to **not** require a Google account to fill out
- [ ] Connect to a **Google Sheet** (Responses tab → green Sheets icon) — all submissions land in one spreadsheet
- [ ] Test the form yourself before sending to a client
