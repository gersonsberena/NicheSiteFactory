## 2. Scope of Work

Service Provider will design, build, and maintain a professional website for Client based on the selected package. The following is included:

### Starter Package Includes:
- 6-section informational website (Home/Hero, About, Services, Testimonials, Contact, Footer)
- Stock photos (until client provides real photos)
- 1 color theme
- Contact form (submissions sent to client's email)
- Calendly / booking button embed (client must provide booking link)
- Mobile-responsive design
- Hosting on Vercel (subdomain or custom domain)
- 1 update request per month (text or photo swap)
- Annual domain renewal management

### Growth Package Includes (everything in Starter, plus):
- Photo gallery (up to 12 client-provided photos)
- Google Analytics tracking
- 1 round of copy edits by Service Provider
- 2 update requests per month

### Pro Package Includes (everything in Growth, plus):
- Video section (YouTube embed — client provides links)
- Instagram handle displayed in footer
- Google Business Profile setup (one-time)
- Monthly analytics email summary
- 4 update requests per month (48-hour turnaround)

## 4. Payment Terms

**4.1 Setup Fee.** The setup fee listed in Section 1 is due in full before any work begins. The setup fee is non-refundable once work has commenced.

**4.2 Monthly Retainer.** The monthly retainer begins on the site launch date and is billed on the same date each month thereafter (e.g., if the site launches on the 15th, retainer bills on the 15th of each subsequent month).

**4.3 Grace Period.** If a monthly payment fails, Client has 7 calendar days to resolve the payment. Service Provider will notify Client of the failure on day 1.

**4.4 Maintenance Mode.** If payment is not received within 7 days of its due date, Service Provider may place the site into maintenance mode (displaying a "temporarily unavailable" message) until payment is received.

**4.5 Termination for Non-Payment.** If payment remains outstanding for 30 calendar days, Service Provider may terminate this agreement and take the site offline. Client's content will be returned per Section 9.

**4.6 Payment Method.** Payment is processed via Stripe. Client authorizes Service Provider to charge the card on file for all recurring monthly fees.

## 5. Client Responsibilities

**5.1 Asset Delivery.** Client agrees to provide all required assets (photos, logo, bio, services list, testimonials, social handles, and booking link) within **5 business days** of signing this agreement and paying the setup fee.

**5.2 Project Clock.** If Client fails to deliver required assets within 5 business days, the project timeline is paused until assets are received. Service Provider is not responsible for launch delays caused by late asset delivery.

**5.3 Content Accuracy.** Client is solely responsible for the accuracy, truthfulness, and legality of all content they provide, including but not limited to bio text, testimonials, service descriptions, pricing, certifications, and credentials.

**5.4 Asset Format.** Client agrees to provide photos in JPEG or PNG format, and logos in PNG format with a transparent background. If a logo is not available, the site will display a text-based placeholder until one is provided.

**5.5 Domain Management.** Client is responsible for purchasing their own domain name (Service Provider recommends Namecheap). Client will provide Service Provider with DNS management access to point the domain to the hosted site. The domain remains the property of Client at all times.

## 10. Cancellation Policy

**10.1 Notice Required.** After the minimum commitment period, either party may cancel with **30 days written notice** sent to the other party's email address listed in this agreement.

**10.2 Final Invoice.** A final monthly invoice covering the 30-day notice period will be sent upon receipt of cancellation notice. Service Provider will continue maintaining the site during the notice period.

**10.3 Site Takedown.** After the notice period and final payment, Service Provider will take the site offline and redirect the subdomain to a neutral page.

**10.4 Content Return.** Per Section 8.3, Client content will be returned within 14 days.

**10.5 Source Code.** Client does not receive the source code upon cancellation under any circumstances.

**10.6 Service Provider Termination.** Service Provider may terminate this agreement immediately and without notice in the event of Client non-payment per Section 4.5, or if Client uses the site for illegal, defamatory, or fraudulent purposes.
