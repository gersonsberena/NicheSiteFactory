## Section 4 — After Launch

### Submitting Update Requests

Once your site is live, submit update requests by emailing gerson.s.berena@gmail.com with:
- Subject: **[YOUR NAME] — Update Request**
- What needs to change and where on the site

We aim to complete updates within **48 hours** of receiving your request (Pro package: 24 hours).

### What Counts as One Update Request

One update request = one consolidated set of changes submitted at the same time. Examples:
- "Update my phone number, swap the hero photo, and add a new testimonial" = **1 request**
- "Add a new service" = **1 request**
- Sending 3 separate emails with one change each = **3 requests**

Bundle your changes before submitting whenever possible.

### Monthly Update Limits

| Package | Requests Included |

| Starter | 1 per month |
| Growth | 2 per month |
| Pro | 4 per month |

Additional requests beyond your monthly limit are $50 each, invoiced before work begins.
